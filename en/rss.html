RSS feed URL

https://kasilovsky.github.io/eirin-report/feed.xml

Add this URL to any RSS reader and you will receive the ten-item digest each day. Opening it directly in a browser shows raw XML — that format is for readers, not for people, which is why this page exists.

How to use it

  1. Install an RSS reader on your phone or computer (Feedly, Inoreader, NetNewsWire, Reeder — any will do)
  2. Choose “Add feed” and paste the URL above
  3. Each day’s digest will arrive in your reader; no need to open this site

Rather not use a reader?

Open the Daily Digest directly, or use the full index to search. The digest page has a “Copy digest” button — paste it straight into your chat.
